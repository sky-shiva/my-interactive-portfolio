About Me

Hey, I'm Shiva — a data science enthusiast who loves building things that actually solve problems.

I'm a 2nd-year B.Tech ECE student with a Generative AI specialization at KLH University, Hyderabad — constantly tinkering with AI, computer vision, and deep learning like they're puzzle pieces waiting to click. I ask "why?" a little too often, break things just to understand them, and build them back cleaner.

"Data, to me, is a story. Code is the pen. And I'm just trying to write something worth reading."

When I'm not debugging or wrestling with Git, I'm building research-grade projects — from low-light image enhancement pipelines to AI safety systems — and applying for internships at IITs to push my limits further.

Curious. Hands-on. Slightly stubborn. Always building.
